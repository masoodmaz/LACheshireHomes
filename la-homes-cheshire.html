Who we are

An independent developer with everything in-house

LA Homes Cheshire Ltd owns all of its sites and carries out every project for itself — no shortcuts, no middlemen. We pride ourselves on completing projects on time with high quality and attention to detail, supported by a dedicated team of professionals: project managers, architects, planners and structural engineers.

From land acquisition through planning, build and landscaping, every stage is handled by the same team — which is how we keep our standards consistent and our promises kept.
